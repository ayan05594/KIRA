 "How is the academic session divided?",
   "Each academic session is divided into two regular semesters: an Autumn Semester (normally July-November) and a Spring Semester (normally December-May). There is also a vacation semester (Summer) of nearly 6-8 weeks duration."


 "What is included in the Academic Calendar?",
   "The Academic Calendar includes the schedule of academic activities for a session, such as dates of registration, examinations, semester breaks, etc."


 "How are course credits assigned?",
   "Credits are assigned based on the following general pattern: one credit for each lecture/tutorial period and half a credit per hour for each laboratory, practical, project, or sessional."


 "What are the credit requirements for a B.Tech. degree?",
   "To qualify for a B.Tech. degree, a student must complete the required credits as prescribed in the curriculum. The general credit requirements are: 155 credits for B.Tech. in an engineering major discipline, 169 credits for B.Tech. with Minor, and 164 credits for B.Tech. with Honors."


 "What is the credit requirement for B.Tech. with a Minor?",
   "The total credit requirement for B.Tech. with a Minor is 169 credits, which includes 155 credits for B.Tech. and 14 additional credits for minor course work."


 "What is the credit requirement for B.Tech. with Honors?",
   "The total credit requirement for B.Tech. with Honors is 164 credits, which includes 155 credits for B.Tech. and 9 additional credits for Honors course work."


 "What are the credit requirements for Computer Science & Communication Engineering and Computer Science & System Engineering?",
   "The credit requirements for Computer Science & Communication Engineering and Computer Science & System Engineering are 156 and 154 credits, respectively."


 "What are the group-wise credit requirements for a B.Tech. degree?",
   "The group-wise credit requirements are: Humanities and Social Sciences (HS) - 12 credits, Basic Sciences (BS) - 30 credits, Engineering Sciences (ES) - 19 credits, Program Core (PC) - 59 credits, Program Electives (PE) - 15 credits, Open Electives - 6 credits, Industry Internship - 4 credits, Graduation Requirements - 155 credits."


 "Who approves the syllabus for a B.Tech. Programme?",
   "Every B.Tech. Programme has a curriculum and syllabus approved by the Academic Council."


 "Who recommends the syllabus for undergraduate courses?",
   "The Board of Studies of the school will discuss and recommend the syllabus of all undergraduate courses offered by the school before submitting it to the Academic Council for consideration and approval."


 "Who holds the copyright for the approved syllabus?",
   "For all approved courses, the copyright will be with KIIT."


 "Are students required to attend all classes?",
   "Yes, students are required to attend all the classes (Lectures, Tutorials, Laboratories, Sessional, Workshops, etc.) for which they have been registered."


 "How is attendance calculated for lectures, tutorials, or practical classes?",
   "For attendance calculation, every scheduled lecture, tutorial, or practical class will count as one unit irrespective of the number of contact hours."


 "What happens if a student's attendance falls below 75%?",
   "A student will be debarred from appearing in an end semester examination if their attendance falls below 75% and will be awarded an ‘F’ grade in that course."


 "What is the procedure for taking a leave of absence for up to two weeks in a semester?",
   "For a leave of absence not exceeding two weeks in a semester, prior application for leave must be submitted to the respective Dean, stating the reasons for the leave and including supporting documents. The Dean may condone the absence if prior application was not possible due to unavoidable reasons."


 "What is the procedure if a leave of absence exceeds two weeks?",
   "If the leave of absence exceeds two weeks, an application for grant of leave must be submitted through the Dean to the Director with supporting documents for sanction of leave."


 "Can leave of absence be counted for attendance?",
   "No, the leave of absence will not be condoned for attendance."


 "Who should a student inform about their absence?",
   "It is the responsibility of the student to inform the Warden of the hostel in which they reside and the concerned instructors regarding their absence before availing the leave."


 "Can any attendance requirements be relaxed?",
   "In exceptional circumstances, the Director, in consultation with the Chairman, Academic Council, may relax any of the attendance requirements."

 "What are the components of theory course assessment?",
   "The theory course assessment consists of the following components:\n1. Activities (30%): Includes surprise quizzes, assignments, projects, practical tests, viva voce, etc.\n2. Mid-semester exam (20%): A one-hour duration examination.\n3. End-semester exam (50%): A three-hour duration examination."


 "What is the general practice for practical course assessments?",
   "The general practice for practical courses follows continuous assessment (60%) and end semester exam (40%)."


 "How is assessment conducted in sessional courses?",
   "In sessional courses, 100% assessment is made in the class."


 "Who decides the detailed break-up of marks for assessment?",
   "The detailed break-up of marks for assessment components is announced and recorded in the first Course Committee meeting. The Dean (Academics) is informed about the scheme of evaluation of a course after the first Course Committee meeting."


 "Can students request make-up activities for missed regular activities?",
   "Yes, students who have missed regular activities for valid reasons may request for make-up activities to the respective course instructor. If the course instructor is satisfied with the provided explanation, they may conduct the make-up activities."


 "Can students request a make-up examination for missed mid-semester, laboratory, or sessional exams?",
   "Yes, students who have missed mid-semester, laboratory, or sessional examinations for valid reasons may request a make-up examination from the course instructor within ten days from the date of the missed test. If the instructor is satisfied with the explanation, they may conduct the make-up exam either during the makeup exam slots in the Academic Calendar or at a convenient time. If not satisfied, the instructor may reject the application."


 "How is the performance of a student in a course denoted?",

 "What does the I-grade indicate?",
   "The I-grade indicates a temporary grade and that a part of the total requirement of the course remains to be completed. It shows that the student has done satisfactory work in all other aspects of the course."


 "What must a student do if they receive an I-grade?",
   "The student must complete the pending requirement within a prescribed date, generally within one month from the start of the following regular semester, to convert the I-grade into a regular grade. If not completed within this period, the I-grade automatically converts to an F grade."


 "What are the letter grades and their corresponding grade points?",
   "The letter grades and their corresponding grade points are:\n- O (90-100): 10 (Outstanding)\n- A (80-89): 9 (Excellent)\n- B (70-79): 8 (Very Good)\n- C (60-69): 7 (Good)\n- D (50-59): 6 (Average)\n- E (40-49): 5 (Below Average)\n- F (Below 40): 0 (Fail)\n- I: Incomplete"


 "What information does the grade sheet issued at the end of the semester contain?",
   "The grade sheet contains:\n1. The course number, course name, and credits for each registered course.\n2. The letter grade obtained in each course.\n3. The total number of credits earned by the student up to that semester.\n4. Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) for that semester.\n5. Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of all courses taken from the first semester."


 "What is included in the Final Grade Report (FGR)?",
   "The Final Grade Report (FGR) contains details of all the courses taken by the student, mentioning the overall CGPA, and is issued along with the degree certificate."


 "What is SGPA and how is it calculated?",
   "Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) is a weighted average of grades in all courses done by the student in a given semester. It is calculated using the formula:\nSGPA = (Σ (C × GP)) / ΣC\nwhere C is the credits of a subject item and GP is the corresponding grade point. SGPA is taken over all registered subjects, including those with F grades, but excluding courses with I grades."

 "What is considered a registered semester in a B.Tech. program?",
   "A semester in which a student has registered for a course will be called a registered semester, subject to the following conditions: \n(a) Only the first and second semesters of an academic year can be registered semesters.
